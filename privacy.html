Privacy Policy

Vice City Vibes · Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Clipper handles. Clipper is the publishing tool that Vice City Vibes uses to post videos and images to its own Facebook Page and Instagram account. It covers the Clipper app registered with Meta (Facebook and Instagram).

What Clipper is

Clipper runs privately on a server operated by the Vice City Vibes team. It is not a public service: people can't sign up or log in to it, and it has no users other than our team. Clipper only publishes to accounts that Vice City Vibes owns or manages and has connected itself.

Information we handle

  • Access tokens for our own accounts. When we connect our Facebook Page and Instagram account, Meta gives Clipper an access token so it can publish on our behalf.
  • Basic account details. The name, ID and profile picture of the accounts we connect, so we can tell them apart.
  • Our own content and its performance. The videos, images and captions we publish, and the view, watch-time and engagement figures of our own posts, so we can see what works.
  • Public video information. To find the best moments in a video, Clipper may read publicly available YouTube data for that video, such as captions, the most-replayed graph, live chat timing and timestamps mentioned in public comments. It uses this only to measure which moments are popular and does not build profiles of the people who wrote them.

Clipper does not collect information about people who visit, follow or interact with our Page or account. It doesn't read private messages, use tracking cookies or analytics, or collect payment information.

How we use it

  • To publish and schedule our own posts and Reels on Facebook and Instagram.
  • To show us the status and performance of what we've published.
  • To choose clip moments and write titles, captions and hashtags for our content.

We don't sell, rent or trade any information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Sharing

Information is shared only as needed to do the work above:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) receives the content we publish to it through its official APIs, under Meta's own privacy policy.
  • AI processing. Video transcripts, frames, titles and topic descriptions are analysed by an AI model to suggest clip moments and write captions. This normally runs on our own server; when an outside AI provider (an OpenAI-compatible service such as DeepSeek) is used instead, it receives only that material. We never send access tokens or personal information about other people.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Storage and security

Everything Clipper stores stays on our team's private server and is not shared with other services. Access tokens and API keys are encrypted, and the app itself is not reachable from the public internet. We keep information only while we use the connected accounts, and we delete it when we disconnect them.

Data deletion

Clipper only holds data for accounts Vice City Vibes owns. To have data removed:

  • Facebook / Instagram: remove the app in Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites). Its access stops at once and the stored token no longer works. We remove the account from Clipper when we disconnect it.
  • Anything else: message us (see below), and we'll delete the data you ask about within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

Children

Clipper isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly handle children's personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
